Xios Desktop (Native iPadOS)

Linux apps as regular iPad windows

xios-native is the flavor with no desktop environment at all: every Linux app becomes a first-class iPadOS window. Each app gets its own home screen icon, opens in its own window, and multitasks with Split View and Slide Over like any other iPad app.

This package installs the ioscd launch daemon and the per-app host that puts each app's window on screen. Tap a Linux app icon on the home screen and it opens; iPadOS itself is the window manager.

It is the lightest flavor and the most iPad-like one. The GNOME and KDE apps in this repo all work as launchable apps here.

Needs iOS 16.0 or newer on a rootless jailbreak (/var/jb).
